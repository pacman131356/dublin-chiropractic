How it works

What is Focused Shock Wave Therapy?

Focused Shock Wave Therapy is a cutting-edge non-invasive treatment method that harnesses the power of shock waves to stimulate the body's natural healing processes. It's often used to treat injured bones, joints, muscles, tendons and other soft tissues. This revolutionary technology is reshaping the way we approach pain management and musculoskeletal disorders.

In the body, these high energy acoustic waves stimulate the cells and the body's intrinsic healing mechanism. By using Extracorporeal Focused Shock Wave Therapy, the wave is transmitted into the body, up to a depth of about 4.7".

Because of the conditions it is used to treat, shock wave therapy is commonly used by athletes or those with 'overuse' injuries, although studies show it can benefit anyone currently undergoing physiotherapy for a range of complaints.

Conditions treated

Fast relief for chronic conditions

With no need for painkillers, shock wave therapy can offer fast pain relief and aid recovery for a range of chronic conditions including:

  • Repetitive strain injury (RSI)
  • Achilles Tendonitis
  • Plantar Fasciitis*
  • Knee and joint arthritis
  • Shin splints
  • Tennis Elbow
  • Medial Tibial Stress Syndrome (otherwise known as shin splints)
  • Hamstring issues
  • Any other chronic soft-tissue joint conditions causing long-term pain

FAQ

Focused Shock Wave Therapy FAQs

Please reach us at DrJamie@DublinFamilyChiropractic.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.

How long does a focused shock wave therapy session typically last?

Each treatment session typically takes between 6 to 10 minutes, making it a quick and efficient option for those with busy schedules.

Is focused shock wave therapy painful?

Patients may experience controlled pain during the session, typically rated around 7/10 on a pain scale of 1-10. It's important to note that the discomfort is short-lived, lasting only for the duration of the treatment and usually relief is experienced at the end of the first treatment session.

How soon can I expect to see results from focused shock wave therapy?

Many patients report noticeable improvements after the first treatment. However, individual responses may vary, and a customized treatment plan will be discussed during your consultation.

What is the success rate of focused shock wave therapy?

Focused shock wave therapy boasts an impressive 87% success rate in addressing various musculoskeletal conditions. This non-invasive approach has been proven effective for many individuals seeking relief from pain.

Are there any negative side effects associated with focused shock wave therapy?

No, focused shock wave therapy is known for its excellent safety profile with minimal to no negative side effects reported. It is a non-surgical and non-pharmaceutical option for those seeking pain relief.

How many sessions of focused shock wave therapy are typically recommended?

The number of sessions needed varies based on the specific condition being treated. Your chiropractor will assess your individual needs during the consultation and recommend a personalized treatment plan to achieve the best results.
