FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Please reach us at DrJamie@DublinFamilyChiropractic.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.

What is chiropractic care?

Chiropractic care is a natural, non-invasive form of healthcare that focuses on the musculoskeletal system, particularly the spine, to improve overall health and wellness.

What conditions can Dublin Family Chiropractic treat?

As a chiropractor, I can treat a wide variety of conditions! Typically, chiropractic is thought of as treating back pain, neck pain, headaches, and joint pain, but did you know we can also adjust extremities like elbows, wrists, shoulders, knees, feet and even your jaw?

Feel free to ask what else chiropractic can help with at your next visit!

What should I expect during my first visit to Dublin Family Chiropractic?

During your first visit, we will discuss your medical history and any current symptoms or concerns you may have. We will also perform a physical exam including range of motion and palpation. If necessary, X-rays will be ordered (but not performed on site). Usually, a chiropractic adjustment will be performed on the first visit, as well!

Can I still receive chiropractic care if I'm pregnant?

Chiropractic is a safe and effective form of treatment during pregnancy. Many women are unaware of the fact that they can be treated with regular chiropractic adjustments throughout their entire pregnancy. Adjustments help relieve lower back ache due to weight gain and/or a shift in the center of gravity from a growing baby. As certain hormones are released, ligaments become less taut and can cause instability, particularly in the pelvic region. Also, new sleeping patterns can sometimes cause neck and upper back/shoulder pain to arise.

Massage therapy is also safe and effective for alleviating the aches and pains associated with pregnancy.

Is Chiropractic (and Massage Therapy) covered under my insurance?

Most insurance plans have chiropractic and massage therapy coverage. The massage therapy is billed under "Physical Therapy" and may only cost you a co-pay or co-insurance (depending on insurance plan and if yearly deductible has been met). You might also be able to use Flexible Spending Accounts or Health Savings Accounts to pay for chiropractic and massage services. For more information, please call our office so we can verify your insurance benefits! 614-764-4001.

* Massage Therapy is NOT billable to Medicare.
